# Privacy Policy

**Solid Waste Management Bot, Nilgiris**
Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Solid Waste Management WhatsApp bot
collects, why, and how it is handled. The bot lets residents report solid waste
problems to the local administration through WhatsApp.

## Information we collect

When you chat with the bot, we collect:

- **Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name**, so we can reply to you.
- **Your language choice** and your place in the conversation.
- **Complaint details** you provide: the type of problem, your block and local
  body, and any description you send.
- **Photos and location** that you choose to send with a complaint.

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or government ID numbers.

## How we use it

We use this information only to:

- receive, record and track your complaint;
- assign the complaint to the right officer and let them contact you;
- send you updates about your complaint;
- improve how complaints are handled.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

- Officers and administrators handling complaints can see the details of
  complaints, including the phone number.
- Our service providers who run the system: Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API) delivers
  messages, and Supabase hosts our database and stored photos.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Complaint records are kept for as long as needed to resolve the complaint and
for official record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

## Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time. To see, correct or delete the
information we hold about you, contact us using the details below.

## Security

Data is stored in access-controlled services, and admin access requires a
login. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect
your information.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
